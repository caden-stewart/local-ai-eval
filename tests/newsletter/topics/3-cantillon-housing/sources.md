# Topic
Who gets the new money first? Credit expansion and housing in Marlow County

# [S1] Marlow County lending and price data (county assessor and bank filings, 2022-2026)
Regional banks expanded mortgage and construction lending in the county by $2.3 billion over four years. In the 11 zip codes containing the busiest lending branches, median home prices rose 18%. In the 14 outer zip codes, they rose 7%. Median household wages across the county rose 3.1%. First-time buyers made up 34% of purchases in 2022 and 22% in 2026. Investor purchases rose from 12% to 21% over the same period.

# [S2] Op-ed by economist Dr. Elena Voss
Voss describes the pattern as a Cantillon effect: new money enters an economy through specific channels, and those closest to it spend it before prices adjust. "The geography is the tell. Demand from newcomers would lift prices broadly. Credit-driven gains cluster around where the credit is created." She calls for banks to publish lending by zip code every quarter so the public can see where new credit is going.

# [S3] Marlow County Renters' Survey 2026
Of 820 renters surveyed, 58% said they had given up on buying a home in the county within five years. Among renters under 35, that figure was 67%. The most common reason given was home prices rising faster than savings (71%), followed by high mortgage rates (19%). The survey's authors caution that it measures expectations and doesn't track whether respondents actually bought homes later.
