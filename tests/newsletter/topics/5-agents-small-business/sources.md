# Topic
What AI agents are really doing for small businesses

# [S1] Tidewater Supply pilot results (plumbing distributor, 60 employees)
Over a six-month pilot, an AI agent drafted quote follow-ups. Average time to first follow-up fell from 26 hours to 3 hours. Quote win rate rose from 31% to 37%, during a season the company describes as strong overall. The system costs about $1,150 per month. In the first month the agent sent incorrect pricing on 14 quotes after reading an outdated price list; the company then required human approval for any message mentioning price, and reports no pricing errors since.

# [S2] Main Street AI Adoption Survey 2026
A survey of 300 US businesses with under 100 employees found 41% use AI tools weekly, but only 12% let AI take actions on their own, such as sending messages or updating records, without a person approving each one. The most common uses were drafting emails (68% of users), summarizing documents (52%) and bookkeeping categorization (33%). The top concern was mistakes reaching customers (57%), followed by data privacy (44%).

# [S3] Practitioner note from an IT consultant serving law and accounting firms
The consultant reports that privacy-sensitive firms increasingly ask about running models on their own hardware so client data never leaves the office. A typical small-office setup she has installed costs between $9,000 and $15,000 up front. Her caution: local models are "fast first-drafters, not final authorities," and every firm she works with keeps a human review step before anything reaches a client or a court.
