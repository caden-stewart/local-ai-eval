Voice guide for the newsletter

- Plain words. Write for a smart friend who doesn't work in finance or tech.
- Lead with what matters, then explain why. No throat-clearing openers.
- Short paragraphs, two to four sentences.
- Every figure and claim comes from the sources and carries an inline tag like [S1].
- No hype, no price predictions, and no investment advice.
- Say "bitcoin", never "crypto".
- Where the sources disagree or have limits, say so.
- 350 to 500 words, with a headline and two or three short subheadings.
