# New client onboarding checklist

Complete these steps in order. The client stays "onboarding" on the roster until every step is done.

1. Run a conflict check against the client roster and note the result in the client file.
2. Send the engagement letter. Margaret Alder signs every engagement letter for the firm.
3. Collect the retainer, if one is required under the fees and billing policy.
4. Send the LedgerVault portal invitation within 2 business days of receiving the signed engagement letter.
5. Request the client's prior 3 years of tax returns through the portal.
6. Hold a kickoff call within 10 business days of the signed engagement letter.
7. Change the client's roster status to "active".
