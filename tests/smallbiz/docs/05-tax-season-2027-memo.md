# Memo: 2027 tax season internal deadlines

From: Margaret Alder, managing partner
To: All staff

Here are this season's internal deadlines. They're earlier than the IRS deadlines on purpose.

- **January 12:** client organizers sent to every individual client.
- **February 15:** completed organizers requested back from clients. Follow up by phone with anyone who hasn't returned theirs.
- **March 15:** every partnership and S-Corp return filed or extended. No exceptions.
- **March 20:** last day we accept new individual clients for this season. Anyone who contacts us after that is offered an extension and a spot after April 15.
- **April 1:** decision deadline for individual extensions. If a return isn't in final review by April 1, file the extension.

## Review queue
Returns go to manager review first, then partner review. Partners won't review a return that hasn't cleared manager review.

## Busy season support
Dinner is provided on Tuesdays and Thursdays from February 1 through April 15 for anyone working past 6:30 PM.
