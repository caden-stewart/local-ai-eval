# Fees and billing policy

## Hourly rates (effective January 1, 2027)
| Role | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Partner | $325 |
| Manager | $240 |
| Senior | $185 |
| Staff accountant | $140 |
| Administrative | $75 |

Recurring clients are usually billed a fixed annual fee instead, shown on the client roster. Work outside the scope of the engagement letter is billed hourly at the rates above.

## Retainers
New clients whose estimated annual fees exceed $5,000 pay a retainer of 50% of the estimated fee before work begins. The retainer is applied to the first invoices.

## Invoices and payment
- Invoices are due within 30 days of the issue date.
- Balances unpaid after 30 days accrue a late fee of 1.5% per month.
- We accept ACH transfers and checks at no charge. Card payments carry a 3% surcharge.
- Filing an extension for an individual return is billed as a flat $150.

## Collections
If an invoice is unpaid after 90 days, the assigned partner decides whether to pause work for that client. Paused clients are marked "paused" on the client roster.
