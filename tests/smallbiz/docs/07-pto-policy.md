# Paid time off policy

## Accrual
- Staff accountants and seniors accrue 15 days of PTO per year.
- Managers and partners accrue 20 days per year.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year. Anything above that is forfeited on January 1.

## Requests
PTO requests go to your manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Blackout periods
No PTO is approved during these periods except for emergencies:
- March 1 through April 15
- September 1 through September 15
- October 1 through October 15

## Busy season Saturdays
Staff who work busy season Saturdays earn one comp day for every 3 Saturdays worked. Comp days must be used by June 30.
