# Document retention policy

We keep records long enough to support clients through audits and to defend our own work, and no longer.

| Record | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- |
| Tax returns and workpapers | 7 years after the filing date |
| Payroll records | 4 years after the payroll year ends |
| Engagement letters | 10 years after the engagement ends |
| Correspondence with tax authorities | 7 years after the matter closes |
| Client-provided original documents | Returned to the client within 60 days of the engagement ending |

## Destruction
Paper records past their retention date are destroyed by a certified shredding service once per quarter. Electronic records are deleted from LedgerVault and from firm backups in the same quarterly cycle.

## Litigation holds
If a client is under audit or involved in litigation, no records for that client are destroyed until the assigned partner lifts the hold in writing.
