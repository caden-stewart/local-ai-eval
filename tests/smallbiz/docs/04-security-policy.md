# Information security policy

## Accounts
- Multi-factor authentication is required on every firm account.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
- Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Client data
- Client documents are exchanged only through LedgerVault. Don't accept tax documents by email, even when a client sends them that way; ask them to re-upload through the portal and delete the email.
- Never send a Social Security number or bank account number by email.
- Personal email accounts may not be used for any client work.

## Devices
- Firm laptops are fully encrypted and may not be shared with family members.
- A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to Kevin Park, Operations Manager, within 1 hour of discovering it's missing.
- Client files may not be stored on USB drives.

## AI tools
- Client data may not be entered into public AI chat services.
- Approved internal AI tools run on firm-controlled hardware and are listed on the operations page.
