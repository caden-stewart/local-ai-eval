# Client memo: Brightwater Landscaping - late S-Corp election

Prepared by: Rosa Delgado
Assigned partner for this matter: Thomas Finch

## Situation
Brightwater Landscaping's owner, Luis Romero, intended the business to be taxed as an S-Corp starting with its first year, but Form 2553 was never filed. The company has filed as an S-Corp for two years regardless.

## Recommendation
Request late election relief using the simplified procedure in Revenue Procedure 2013-30. Relief under that procedure must be requested within 3 years and 75 days of the intended effective date, and Brightwater is still inside that window.

## Next steps
1. Confirm the owner's consent and that every return was filed consistently as an S-Corp.
2. Prepare Form 2553 with the reasonable-cause statement.
3. Thomas Finch reviews and signs before filing.
