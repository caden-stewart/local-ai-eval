# Alder & Finch CPAs - Firm overview (internal)

Alder & Finch is a public accounting firm serving small businesses, nonprofits and individuals. We prepare tax returns, run bookkeeping and payroll for small clients, and provide business advisory work such as entity selection and succession planning.

## Offices
- Main office: 410 Alder Street, Suite 300. Reception, all partners, and the records room.
- Harbor office: 88 Harbor Street, Unit B. Bookkeeping and payroll team.

## Hours
- Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM at both offices.
- Busy season Saturdays: the main office is open 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays from February 1 through April 15. The Harbor office stays closed on Saturdays.

## Partners
- Margaret Alder, managing partner. Firm operations, nonprofit clients, final sign-off on engagement letters.
- Thomas Finch, partner. Individual and trust tax, IRS representation.
- Rosa Delgado, partner. Business advisory, S-Corp and partnership clients.
